Our Commitment

At REX STORE, we are committed to ensuring that our website and services are accessible to all customers, including those with disabilities. We strive to provide an inclusive shopping experience that accommodates the diverse needs of our global clientele.

We continuously work to improve the accessibility of our digital platforms by following the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards and implementing best practices in web accessibility.

Our team regularly reviews our website and makes enhancements to ensure we provide the best possible experience for all users, regardless of their abilities or the assistive technologies they may use.

Accessibility Features

Features we've implemented to enhance the accessibility of our website.

Visual Accessibility

Our website is designed with adequate color contrast, resizable text, and clear visual hierarchy to ensure readability for users with visual impairments.

Keyboard Navigation

All interactive elements on our website are accessible via keyboard navigation, with visible focus indicators to help users track their position on the page.

Screen Reader Compatibility

We use semantic HTML and ARIA attributes to ensure our content is properly interpreted by screen readers and other assistive technologies.

Accessibility Guidelines

Standards we follow to ensure our website is accessible to all users.

WCAG 2.1 Compliance

We strive to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards, which cover a wide range of recommendations for making web content more accessible.

  • Perceivable: Information and user interface components must be presentable to users in ways they can perceive.
  • Operable: User interface components and navigation must be operable by all users.
  • Understandable: Information and the operation of the user interface must be understandable.
  • Robust: Content must be robust enough to be interpreted reliably by a wide variety of user agents, including assistive technologies.

Supported Assistive Technologies

Our website is designed to work with various assistive technologies that users may rely on to navigate the web.

  • Screen Readers: Compatible with popular screen readers such as JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver, and TalkBack.
  • Speech Recognition: Support for voice commands and speech recognition software.
  • Screen Magnifiers: Compatible with screen magnification software for users with low vision.
  • Alternative Input Devices: Support for various input methods beyond standard keyboard and mouse.
