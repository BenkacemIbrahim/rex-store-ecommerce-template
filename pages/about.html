Our Story

Founded in 2010, REX STORE began with a simple vision: to create a curated shopping destination for discerning gentlemen and their children, offering the finest fashion from the world's most prestigious brands.

What started as a small boutique in Paris has grown into an internationally recognized luxury retailer, while maintaining our commitment to personalized service and uncompromising quality.

Our carefully selected collections represent the pinnacle of craftsmanship, design, and style, ensuring that every item that bears the REX STORE name meets our exacting standards.
