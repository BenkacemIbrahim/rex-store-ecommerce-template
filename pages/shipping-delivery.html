At REX STORE, we offer a variety of shipping options to ensure your premium fashion items reach you in perfect condition and in a timeframe that suits your needs.

We partner with trusted global carriers to provide secure and reliable delivery services across the world, with special care taken for our luxury items.

All orders are carefully packaged in our signature boxes with premium protective materials to ensure your items arrive in pristine condition.

Shipping Policies

Important information about our shipping and delivery policies.

Order Processing

All orders are processed within 24-48 hours of being placed, excluding weekends and holidays. You will receive an order confirmation email once your order has been processed.

For made-to-order or personalized items, please allow additional processing time as indicated on the product page.

Shipping Destinations

We ship to most countries worldwide. During checkout, you can select your country to see if we deliver to your location and to calculate shipping costs.

Please note that for some countries, additional customs duties and taxes may apply, which are the responsibility of the recipient.

Tracking & Insurance

All shipments include tracking information that will be sent to you via email once your order has been shipped. You can also view tracking information in your account under "Order History."

All orders are fully insured against loss or damage during transit at no additional cost to you.

Secure

Delivery

Delivery Information

Delivery Confirmation

All deliveries require a signature upon receipt to ensure your items are safely delivered to the intended recipient. If you're not available to sign, the carrier will leave a notice with instructions for rescheduling or pickup.

Address Accuracy

Please ensure your shipping address is accurate and complete. REX STORE is not responsible for delays or non-delivery due to incorrect address information.

Delivery Delays

While we strive to meet all estimated delivery timeframes, delays may occasionally occur due to customs clearance, weather conditions, or other circumstances beyond our control. We will keep you informed of any significant delays affecting your order.

International Shipping

Important information for our international customers.

Customs & Duties

International orders may be subject to customs duties and taxes imposed by the destination country. These charges are the responsibility of the recipient and are not included in our shipping fees.

  • Customs duties are determined by the customs authorities of your country based on the value, origin, and type of item.
  • Payment of these fees may be required before delivery can be completed.
  • REX STORE cannot predict or influence the amount of customs duties that may be charged.

International Returns

We accept returns from international customers within our standard 30-day return window. Please note the following special considerations for international returns:

  • Return shipping costs for international orders are the responsibility of the customer, except in cases of defective items.
  • Any customs duties or taxes paid on the original shipment are typically non-refundable by customs authorities.
  • Please contact our Customer Service team for detailed instructions on international returns.
