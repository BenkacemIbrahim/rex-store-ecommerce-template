Our Return Policy

At REX STORE, we stand behind the quality of our products and want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. We offer a straightforward 30-day return policy for all unworn, unwashed, and unaltered items with original tags attached and in their original packaging.

Returns initiated within 30 days of delivery are eligible for a full refund to the original payment method. For exchanges, we'll gladly help you find the perfect size or alternative item.

Please note that certain items, such as personalized products, intimate apparel, and final sale items, are not eligible for return due to hygiene reasons or as specified at the time of purchase.

Return Process

Follow these simple steps to return or exchange your items.

1

Initiate Your Return

Log in to your account, go to "Order History," select the order containing the item(s) you wish to return, and click "Return Items." Alternatively, contact our Customer Service team for assistance.

2

Package Your Items

Carefully pack the item(s) in their original packaging with all tags attached. Print the return label provided and attach it to your package. Include the completed return form in your package.

3

Ship Your Return

Drop off your package at any authorized shipping location. Keep your receipt as proof of return. Once we receive and process your return, you'll receive a confirmation email with refund details.

Return Policy Details

Important information about our return and exchange policies.

Return Timeframe

  • 30-Day Window: Returns must be initiated within 30 days of delivery date.
  • Processing Time: Returns typically take 3-5 business days to process once received.
  • Refund Timeline: Refunds will be issued to your original payment method and may take an additional 5-10 business days to appear on your statement, depending on your financial institution.
  • Extended Holiday Returns: Special extended return periods may apply during holiday seasons. Please check our website for current policies.

Return Conditions

  • Item Condition: Items must be unworn, unwashed, and unaltered with all original tags attached.
  • Original Packaging: Items should be returned in their original packaging, including dust bags, boxes, and authenticity cards where applicable.
  • Non-Returnable Items: Personalized items, intimate apparel, final sale items, and items marked as non-returnable at the time of purchase cannot be returned.
  • Gift Returns: Gift recipients may return items for store credit or exchange without the original receipt.

Shipping & Fees

  • Return Shipping: A flat fee of €10 will be deducted from your refund amount to cover return shipping costs.
  • Free Return Shipping: Return shipping is complimentary for exchanges, defective items, or incorrect items shipped.
  • International Returns: International customers are responsible for return shipping costs and any applicable customs duties or taxes.
  • Restocking Fee: No restocking fees apply for standard returns within the 30-day window.

Exchanges

  • Size or Color Exchange: Exchanges for a different size or color of the same item are processed free of charge.
  • Different Item Exchange: If you wish to exchange for a different item, we will process a return and a new purchase.
  • Price Difference: If the exchange item costs more than the original, you will be charged the difference. If it costs less, you will be refunded the difference.
  • Availability: Exchanges are subject to availability. If the requested item is out of stock, we will issue a refund or store credit.

Defective Items

We take great pride in the quality of our products. However, if you receive a defective item, please contact our Customer Service team within 48 hours of delivery.

For defective items, we offer the following options:

  • Full refund including all shipping costs
  • Free replacement of the same item
  • Store credit with an additional 10% bonus

Please provide photos of the defect when contacting our Customer Service team to expedite the process.
