Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our products, services, and policies.

Orders & Shipping

Standard shipping typically takes 3-5 business days within Europe and 7-10 business days for international orders. Express shipping is available for 1-2 business day delivery within Europe and 3-5 business days internationally.

Please note that delivery times may vary during peak seasons or due to customs clearance for international shipments.

Yes, we offer complimentary standard shipping on all orders over €500. For orders under €500, standard shipping fees apply based on your location:

  • France: €10
  • Europe: €15
  • International: €25

Yes, once your order has been shipped, you will receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can use this number to track your package on our website or directly through the carrier's website.

You can also view your order status and tracking information in your account under "Order History".

We ship to most countries worldwide. During checkout, you can select your country to see if we deliver to your location and to calculate shipping costs.

Please note that for some countries, additional customs duties and taxes may apply, which are the responsibility of the recipient.

Returns & Exchanges

We offer a 30-day return policy for all unworn, unwashed, and unaltered items with original tags attached and in their original packaging. Returns initiated within 30 days of delivery are eligible for a full refund to the original payment method.

Please note that certain items, such as personalized products or intimate apparel, are not eligible for return due to hygiene reasons.

To initiate a return or exchange:

  1. Log in to your account and go to "Order History"
  2. Select the order containing the item(s) you wish to return
  3. Click "Return Items" and follow the instructions
  4. Print the return label and attach it to your package
  5. Drop off the package at your nearest authorized shipping location

If you prefer, you can also contact our Customer Service team for assistance with your return or exchange.

Once we receive your return, it typically takes 3-5 business days to inspect and process. After processing, refunds will be issued to your original payment method and may take an additional 5-10 business days to appear on your statement, depending on your financial institution.

For exchanges, once your return is processed, we will ship the new item to you. You will receive a confirmation email with tracking information when your exchange is shipped.

For standard returns, a flat fee of €10 will be deducted from your refund amount to cover return shipping costs. However, return shipping is complimentary for:

  • Exchanges for a different size or color
  • Items that arrived damaged or defective
  • Incorrect items shipped

Products & Sizing

We provide detailed size guides for all our products. To find the right size:

  1. Go to the product page of the item you're interested in
  2. Click on the "Size Guide" link near the size selection options
  3. Follow the measuring instructions and compare your measurements to our size chart

If you're between sizes, we generally recommend sizing up for a more comfortable fit. For specific sizing advice, please contact our Customer Service team.

Yes, we guarantee that all products sold on REX STORE are 100% authentic. We source our products directly from the brands or their authorized distributors. Each item undergoes a rigorous authentication process before being listed on our website.

All products come with original brand packaging, tags, and authenticity cards where applicable. We have zero tolerance for counterfeit products.

Care instructions vary by product and material. Detailed care information is provided on each product page and on the care label attached to the item. Generally, we recommend:

  • Following the specific care instructions on the product label
  • Storing items properly when not in use
  • Using specialized cleaning services for luxury items
  • Avoiding exposure to direct sunlight, moisture, and extreme temperatures

For specific care advice for your purchase, please contact our Customer Service team.

Yes, we offer professional alteration services for purchases made at REX STORE. Basic alterations such as hemming and sleeve adjustments are complimentary for full-price items. More complex alterations are available for an additional fee.

We also offer select customization services for certain products, including monogramming and personalization. These services are available for an additional fee and may extend the delivery time.

To inquire about alterations or customization options, please contact our Customer Service team or visit our store in person.

Account & Payment

We accept the following payment methods:

  • Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
  • PayPal
  • Apple Pay
  • Google Pay
  • Bank Transfer (for orders over €1,000)

All transactions are processed securely using industry-standard encryption technology.

Creating an account is simple:

  1. Click on the account icon in the top right corner of our website
  2. Select "Create Account" from the dropdown menu
  3. Enter your email address and create a password
  4. Fill in your personal information
  5. Click "Create Account" to complete the process

You can also create an account during the checkout process by selecting "Create an account" before completing your purchase.

Yes, your payment information is completely secure. We use industry-standard SSL encryption to protect all sensitive data. We are PCI DSS compliant, which means we adhere to the highest security standards for handling credit card information.

We never store your full credit card details on our servers. For recurring customers who choose to save their payment information, we use tokenization technology, which replaces sensitive data with non-sensitive equivalents that cannot be exploited even in the unlikely event of a data breach.

Yes, we offer a guest checkout option that allows you to place an order without creating an account. During checkout, simply proceed without logging in or creating an account.

However, creating an account offers several benefits:

  • Faster checkout for future purchases
  • Order tracking and history
  • Saved shipping addresses and payment methods
  • Access to exclusive offers and early sales
  • Ability to create and manage wishlists

Services

Yes, we offer complimentary personal shopping services both in-store and virtually. Our experienced stylists can help you find the perfect items based on your preferences, style, and needs.

To book a personal shopping appointment:

  • In-store: Visit our store in Paris or call +33 (0)1 42 61 10 10
  • Virtual: Book through our website or contact our Customer Service team

Appointments typically last 60-90 minutes and are tailored to your specific requirements.

We offer complimentary gift wrapping for all purchases. During checkout, you can select the gift wrapping option and add a personalized message that will be printed on a premium card.

Our signature gift wrapping includes:

  • Premium black matte paper with gold accents
  • Satin ribbon
  • REX STORE wax seal
  • Personalized message card
  • Dust bag or box (depending on the item)

For special requests or custom gift wrapping, please contact our Customer Service team.

Yes, we offer both physical and digital gift cards in denominations ranging from €100 to €5,000. Gift cards are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase and can be used both online and in-store.

Physical gift cards are presented in premium packaging and can be shipped to your address or directly to the recipient. Digital gift cards are delivered via email and can be personalized with a message.

To purchase a gift card, visit the "Gift Cards" section of our website or contact our Customer Service team.

Yes, we welcome in-store appointments for a more personalized shopping experience. You can book appointments for:

  • Personal shopping and styling
  • Product consultations
  • Alterations and customization
  • Special orders

To book an appointment, you can use our online booking system, call our store directly at +33 (0)1 42 61 10 10, or email us at appointments@rexstore.com with your preferred date and time.
